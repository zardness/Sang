The History of Football

Football is known today as the sport enjoyed by the largest number of people in the world. More than 200 countries have joined FIFA, and the World Cup has become a flagship international sporting event watched by billions of people around the world. However, it took a long time for football to take on its current form. The history of football is not merely the development of a single sport, but is also closely connected to the processes through which human culture, society, and the economy change.

The origins of soccer can be found in ancient societies. Ball-kicking games of the kind have existed for a long time in many regions around the world. One of the oldest records is China’s “chouji.” Chouzi was widely popularized around the 2nd century BCE during the Han dynasty and was used for military training and physical conditioning. The participants had to kick a leather ball with their feet and get it into a designated target. Because it does not use the hands like modern soccer, many scholars regard chouji as one of the early forms of soccer.

In ancient Greece, a game called “episkyros” was popular, and in Rome, a ball game called “harpastum” was widely known. These games differed in rules from modern football, but shared the common feature of competing using a ball. Even in medieval Europe, ball games played at the village level were often held. However, the games at the time had almost no rules, and there were no limits on the number of participants. At times, hundreds of people participated in the game all at once, and injuries were also frequent due to excessive competition.

The foundation of modern football was laid in 19th-century Britain. Since the Industrial Revolution, British society changed rapidly, and the importance of school education and sports activities was further emphasized. At the time, many schools in England enjoyed various ball games, but because each school had different rules, it was difficult to play matches against one another. One school allowed players to catch the ball with their hands, while another school enforced a rule that only used the feet.

To resolve this confusion, in London in 1863 representatives from various football organizations and schools gathered to establish the Football Association. This organization is regarded as the world’s first football association today. They systematically organized the rules of the game and established provisions prohibiting the use of hands. In this process, games involving handling the ball with the hands developed into rugby, while games played with the feet as the main focus separated into soccer.

After the official rules were established, football began to grow rapidly. In Britain, regional football clubs were established one after another, and regular tournaments began to be held. In 1871, the FA Cup, the oldest football competition in the world, was established. Afterwards, football spread throughout Britain along with the development of the railways. Railways made the movement of players and spectators more convenient and became a catalyst for further energizing football matches.

In the late 19th century, football spread internationally as British sailors, merchants, and soldiers traveled to various countries around the world. In South America, football developed rapidly under the influence of British companies and workers. In particular, Brazil and Argentina developed their own distinct football cultures and grew into world-class football powerhouses. Europe also actively embraced football, and countries such as France, Germany, Italy, and Spain became central players leading world football while developing their own leagues.

In 1904, the International Football Federation (FIFA) was founded in Paris, France. FIFA was established to unify the rules of international competition and to organize international tournaments systematically. At first, only a few countries participated, but over time the number of member countries increased significantly. The launch of FIFA became an important turning point in the growth of football into a global sport.

In 1930, the first FIFA World Cup was held in Uruguay. At the time, there weren’t many participating countries, but the conference attracted great interest. With Uruguay claiming the inaugural championship, football further strengthened its position as an international sport. Since then, the World Cup has developed into the world’s largest sporting event, held every four years. Legendary players such as Brazil’s Pelé, Argentina’s Diego Maradona, Portugal’s Cristiano Ronaldo, and Argentina’s Lionel Messi rewrote the history of world football.

It is known that soccer was first introduced to our country in the late 1880s. At that time, as foreign missionaries and teachers introduced soccer to schools, a soccer culture was formed centered around the students. Later, as the National Sports Festival and high school soccer became more active, soccer began to establish itself as a popular sport. In 1983, professional soccer was launched, and as a result, the domestic football industry began to grow in earnest.

One of the most important moments in the history of Korean football is the 2002 Korea-Japan World Cup. The South Korean national team, based on outstanding performance, became the first Asian nation to reach the World Cup semifinals. This achievement deeply moved the public and became a major turning point in greatly increasing interest in football. Since then, Korean football has continuously developed its youth system and professional league, strengthening its international competitiveness.

Today, soccer has developed beyond a simple sport into a culture and an industry. Football serves to bring people together beyond nation, race, and language. It is also having a major impact not only on athletes’ skills, tactics, and teamwork, but also on the economy, media, and tourism industries. Going forward, football will continue to evolve as a sport loved by people around the world, embracing new technologies and cultures in line with changing times.
